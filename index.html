BIOKID-PGx

Predicting response to anti-TNF therapy in pediatric inflammatory bowel disease, so every child receives the most suitable treatment.

The project

Why it matters

Pediatric inflammatory bowel disease (pIBD) is a chronic condition that can strongly affect the health and quality of life of children and their families. Anti-TNF biological therapies have greatly improved its management, but not every patient responds, and today it is difficult to predict which therapy will work best for each individual child.

The goal

Develop and validate a multi-omics-based risk score that predicts response to anti-TNF therapy.

The clinical benefit

Support personalized treatment decisions and reduce ineffective treatments in children with IBD.

The digital tool

An AI-enabled platform to bring the predictive score into everyday clinical practice.

Approach

One integrated picture of each child

Genomic, transcriptomic, metabolomic and microbiome data are combined with clinical information using advanced bioinformatics and machine-learning methods. The aim is to identify and validate biomarkers linked to treatment response and merge them into one personalized predictive score, tested in international cohorts of children with IBD.
